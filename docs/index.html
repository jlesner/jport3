I'm a Computer Science Ph.D. student at UC Santa Barbara, advised by Xifeng Yan. I apply AI/ML to real-world problems, from LLM-aided database verification to accessible interfaces for retinal implants, and I now study how large language models and domain-specific languages such as SQL can help each other. My aim is to close the gap between what AI can do and what we can trust it to do.
